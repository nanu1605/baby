Haan yaar, weekend “ghar pe chill” ke liye kuch mast plans—choose kar lo mood ke hisaab se 😄

## 1) Movie + Comfort Night (Low effort, High vibe)
- 1/2 movie + 1 series (mix)
- Snacks: popcorn + fries/chaat/whatever
- Light: dim + playlist (lofi/feel-good)
**Pro tip:** “theme” set karo—romcom night / sci‑fi night / horror (agar vibe ho).

## 2) Game Night + Snacks Tournament
- FIFA/EA FC, Valorant custom, Mario/any casual, ya board games
- Winner gets “boss snack” (like brownies/ice cream)
**Pro tip:** 3 rounds max, warna weekend “overgaming” ho jayega 😅

## 3) Cooking Challenge (2 hours fun)
- Pair game: tum + house partner (or friend/parent)
- Theme: “One ingredient, multiple dishes”  
  Example: paneer/chicken/veg → paneer tikka + sandwich / chicken fried rice + wrap
**End:** sab milke taste test + ranking.

## 4) Cleaning + Reset + Mini Self-care
- 30–40 min “speed clean”
- Then: shower + skincare / hair oil / face pack
- Evening: cozy tea/coffee + music
**Result:** ghar bhi fresh, mood bhi.

## 5) Photo/Memory + Skill Night
- Old photos arrange, ya phone videos export + montage
- Skill: something quick like Canva posters, editing, language practice, or learning a song on guitar
**Pro tip:** 1 hour learn + 30 min output (small but satisfying).

## 6) “Outdoor but at Home” (Balcony/terrace vibe)
- Blankets + string lights
- Drinks + snacks
- Sunset/late evening walk around building + return
**Cozy + low cost.**
